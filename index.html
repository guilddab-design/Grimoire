Casting

Roll 2D + Magic(Skill) against the spell's difficulty. Magic(Skill) is one of the traditions: Miracle, Heroism, Alchemy, Divination, Shamanism, Witchcraft, Hedge Magic, Hermetic Magecraft, Weapon Arts, Fae Magic, Sensing, Shadow Magic, Anti-Magic, Precognition, Elementalist.

If the spell's method doesn't fit the tradition used, the difficulty rises by +2.

Reagents are consumed when the spell is cast. Catalysts are not.

Effects

The effects define the spell. Target, range and duration don't: a self-targeted heal cast on a friend is the same spell with Target other +1. Add or remove an effect and it's a new spell.

Effect Level is the sum of effect values: 1 per Minor, 1 per die for a Major (2D = 2). A spell costs 1 MP per Effect Level.

Reading the effect scale

  • Attribute change means damage or healing, since damage in Traveller lands on the physical attributes. 1D of damage or healing is a Minor. A Major starts at 2D.
  • Healing can't raise an attribute past its natural value. Raising an attribute is a separate buff: each +1 point is its own Minor, and buffs can exceed natural value.
  • 1D of attribute change is worth ±1 to a skill. Six points of armor is one Minor.
  • Armor applies separately to each effect of a spell.
  • At Magic 5, a Major costs +1 per die instead of +2. A skill at 5 can also be used in unrealistic ways: Stealth 5 from magic can cross an open field unseen.

Minor effects

Grant a boon. Make something usable as a reaction. Heal 1D or cure a minor ailment. 1D attribute change. Raise or lower an attribute by 1. Six points of armor. Harmless sensory illusion. Conjure one size-1 creature. Repair a broken (not destroyed) object. Sense danger, a natural thing, or tomorrow's weather with certainty. Attribute shift: use a different attribute for a skill. Undo natural bonds such as knots. Lull a creature that is drowsy, already asleep, or willing to sleep. Change your shape and appearance (bonus or penalty by size). +1.5 m movement. Protect a creature from normal weather.

If it isn't on the list and doesn't seem game-breaking, allow it as a Minor.

Major effects

Inflict a bane. Force an awake, alert creature to sleep. Turn an action into a minor action. Any attribute change of 2D or more. Summoning and creation (below).

Magic points

  • Maximum MP = the sum of all your Magic skills + 2 × INT DM + END DM, minimum 1.
  • Weekly recovery: at the start of each week roll 1D + END DM and regain it evenly across the week, rounding down each day.
  • Rest: a full day of rest restores 1 + INT DM, on top of weekly recovery.
  • Spent MP doesn't recover while any of your non-permanent effects are still active.
  • Permanent effects lock MP. A permanent buff removes its Effect Level from your maximum for as long as it lasts. Restorative spells (healing, repair, creation) resolve once and lock nothing.
  • Going below 0 MP forces you to end one of your active non-permanent effects, if you have any. Spend Luck to avoid this, once per reduction.
  • Extra MP can go two ways: lower the DC (see the table above), or empower the spell, adding +1 Effect Level per MP to make it harder to resist. The higher Effect Level also counts against your skill, so empowering past your skill raises the DC.

Resisting

A target of an unwanted spell may resist as a reaction: 2D + Magic(Skill) ≥ Effect Level + 8. Spending 1 MP gives the roll a boon.

Anyone untrained in magic rolls at −3, so ordinary people almost never shake off a spell. That's intended: mundanes are highly vulnerable to magic.

Spontaneous and practiced spells

  • Spontaneous spells are built on the spot. If one fails, it can't be cast again with the same Magic(Skill).
  • You know 1 + EDU DM practiced spells. Each is written out with the formula and grants +2 DM to cast.
  • A spontaneous spell that succeeds may be taken as a practiced spell.
  • Practiced spells can also be learned from a text, by rote, or from a teacher. Learning this way ignores the failure lockout.
  • Rote: each month of work on a spell gives +1 on its first casting.

Ongoing effects

  • Buffs and debuffs don't stack on attributes, skills or most other effects.
  • Remove an existing buff before replacing it. Debuffs apply the highest value.
  • Ending your own effect takes one action per Effect Level. Powerful spells take a long time to unravel.
  • Effects run without concentration. Adding a concentration clause to a group of effects is itself an effect: Minor, or Major if the group contains a Major.

Summoning

Wildlife

One Major summons creatures up to the cow-or-horse row; larger animals need a second Major. Summoned creatures aren't controlled. Control is another Major. Don't forget duration.

Magical creatures

A magical creature must be bound by a pact first. Summoning a pact-bound creature is then a Major.

Forced pact: a Major, targeting other, with Effect Level equal to the creature's Magic(Skill). The creature may resist as normal.

Creation

  • Transformation: changing one substance into another is a Major. The Effect Level needed rises by 1 per 10 CU of value. No value may be gained in the change.
  • Creation from nothing: a Major whose Effect Level must equal the item's CU value.
